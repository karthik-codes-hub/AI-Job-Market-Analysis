# 🤖 AI Job Market Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze the evolving AI job market and provide insights into **job demand, AI skills, salaries, career opportunities, industries, companies, and employment trends**.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

The AI job market is growing rapidly, but it can be difficult for students, job seekers, and professionals to understand:

- Which AI skills are most in demand?
- Which AI roles offer higher salaries?
- How does salary change with experience?
- Which industries and companies provide the most opportunities?
- Where are AI jobs concentrated globally?
- What type of work arrangements are available?
- How are LLM-related roles changing the job market?

Raw job-posting data contains these insights, but extracting meaningful information from it manually can be time-consuming.

---

## 💡 Solution

The **AI Job Market Analytics Dashboard** transforms raw job-market data into an interactive and easy-to-understand analytics platform.

Users can explore the AI job market through multiple analytical pages covering:

-  Executive market overview
-  AI skills & technology demand
-  Salary & career insights
-  Industry & company opportunities
-  Global job distribution

Interactive filters allow users to analyze the data based on **job category, industry, experience level, country, salary tier, year, and LLM roles**.

##  How This Dashboard Helps

###  Students & Freshers

Understand which **AI skills and technologies** are currently demanded and identify potential career paths.

###  Job Seekers

Compare **job categories, salaries, industries, experience levels, and job requirements** to make better career decisions.

###  AI Professionals

Track the demand for technologies such as **Python, PyTorch, SQL, and other AI-related skills** across different job categories.

###  Recruiters & Organizations

Gain insights into **AI talent demand, salary levels, industries, company sizes, and work arrangements**.

###  Career Advisors

Use data-driven insights to guide students and professionals toward **relevant skills and career opportunities**.
